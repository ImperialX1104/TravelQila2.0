Visa document checklist

The exact checklist depends on your passport, destination, visa category and personal circumstances. These are common document categories to discuss with our team, not a universal or exhaustive list.

Passport

Valid passport and any required previous passports

Application details

Completed application and required photographs

Travel plan

Proposed itinerary and travel dates, where required

Stay details

Accommodation or invitation information, where required

Financial evidence

Financial documents as specified by the destination authority

Supporting papers

Employment, business, study or other purpose-related evidence

Always follow the latest checklist and instructions published by the relevant embassy, consulate or official visa portal. Travel Qila cannot guarantee visa approval or processing time.
