Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy explains how Travel Qila ("Travel Qila", "we", "us" or "our") handles information when you visit or use this website. It is intended to describe the information practices relevant to the website and the services made available through it.

Please read this policy carefully. By using the website, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will seek that consent separately; simply browsing this page does not constitute consent to optional tracking or marketing.

1. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the features you choose to use and the information you submit. This may include:

  • Contact and account information: such as your name, email address, telephone number, login credentials and profile details, if you register or contact us.
  • Travel and enquiry information: such as destinations, travel dates, package preferences, booking or enquiry details and messages you submit.
  • Payment-related information: if paid services are offered, payment details are handled by the payment service provider involved. We should only receive the payment status and limited transaction details that provider makes available to us. Do not send full card numbers or security codes to us by email or contact form.
  • Location information: if you choose to provide your location or enable a location-based feature, the website or relevant service may process location information to provide that feature.
  • Technical and usage information: such as IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, approximate access times and diagnostic information, where collected by our hosting, security or analytics tools.
  • Communications: the content of messages, feedback and support requests you send to us.

Please provide only information that is necessary for your enquiry or use of the relevant feature.

2. How we may use information

Depending on the feature and the applicable legal basis, information may be used to:

  • respond to enquiries and provide customer support;
  • create and administer accounts, where account functionality is available;
  • process travel requests, reservations or bookings that you ask us to arrange;
  • send service-related updates about an enquiry or booking;
  • maintain, troubleshoot, secure and improve the website;
  • detect misuse, fraud, unauthorised access or other security issues;
  • meet legal obligations and protect our rights; and
  • send promotional communications only where permitted and, when required, with your consent. You can opt out of marketing messages.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. The website or its service providers may use strictly necessary cookies to support core functions, remember preferences, protect the website or maintain a session. Analytics or advertising cookies should only be enabled where they are actually used and where required consent has been obtained.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website functionality. If this website uses a cookie consent tool, use that tool to change your optional cookie preferences.

4. When information may be shared

We do not describe personal information as being sold. Information may be disclosed only as appropriate for the purposes explained in this policy, including to:

  • Service providers: hosting, website maintenance, customer support, communications, analytics or security providers engaged to support the website, if used.
  • Travel and booking partners: hotels, transport operators, tour providers or other suppliers, when sharing is necessary to respond to or fulfil a travel request or booking you make.
  • Payment providers: payment processors involved in a transaction, if online payments are enabled.
  • Authorities or other parties: where disclosure is required by law, legal process or a valid request, or is reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety and security.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, restructuring or transfer of business, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable law.

The actual recipients depend on the integrations and services configured on the live website. We should update this section if those providers or practices change.

5. Data retention and security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to provide a requested service, resolve disputes, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the relevant purpose.

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please contact us promptly if you believe your interaction with the website has exposed your information.

6. Your choices and privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, withdraw consent, object to or restrict certain processing, or raise a complaint with a relevant authority.

You may also opt out of promotional emails by using the unsubscribe method in the message, where provided, or by contacting us. Service and transaction messages may still be sent when necessary.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details in the Contact us section. We may need to verify your identity before responding and will handle requests in accordance with applicable law.

7. Children's privacy

This website is not intended to invite children to submit personal information independently. Where a service involves a child traveller, a parent or lawful guardian should provide the necessary information and any consent required by law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. International transfers

Some website or service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you are located. Where this occurs, we will take steps required by applicable data protection law to protect the information.

9. Third-party websites and services

The website may contain links to third-party websites or services. Those services operate under their own privacy notices and practices. We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of third-party websites, so please review their policies before providing information to them.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the website, our practices or legal requirements. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically.
