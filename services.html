GOOD TO KNOW

Flight booking FAQs

A few helpful details before you send your request.

Are the fares and flight timings live?

No. The current search uses illustrative mock data only. Flight numbers, timings, fares, durations and baggage details are examples for testing the website and are not verified airline inventory. Our team must confirm a current fare and availability before any booking.

Does submitting this form confirm my ticket?

No. This is an enquiry only. Your booking is confirmed only after you review the final itinerary, fare rules and payment instructions and receive confirmation from Travel Qila.

Can I request flights for children and infants?

Yes. Select the correct passenger counts. Our team may contact you for passenger names and dates of birth before ticketing.

Can I ask for a specific airline or baggage allowance?

Yes. Add your preferred airline or baggage requirements in the additional preferences field.
